# Proposal: cybersecurity career discovery and assessed portfolio pilot

Prepared: 8 November 2026. Discussion draft for a university or local technology-community partner. Subject to product readiness, reviewer availability, and a written scope; this is not an active program or signed agreement.

## Purpose

Help up to 15 adult beginners choose a cybersecurity direction, complete practical work, receive assessed portfolio feedback, and leave with a specific next step toward relevant work.

## The proposed collaboration

Ask the partner to nominate one coordinator, arrange one 45-minute career orientation, and circulate an approved invitation to an opt-in pilot. Areté would supply the career quiz, complete published pathways, evidence-based tasks, assigned reviewers, support, and an aggregate delivery report.

The quiz recommends blue-team/SOC foundations, red-team/ethical testing foundations, GRC, or application security from interests and practical judgment. The career map also explains purple-team progression and other specialties, including the distinction between grey-box testing and grey-hat terminology. Foundations gaps produce a preparation bridge. Learners can change paths. Recruit only after every offered path and its review capacity meet the release gates in the [implementation plan](career-pathway-implementation-plan.md). Before then, a partner can host a career-explorer session: learners take the quiz and are pointed at the existing security rooms, with no enrollment or assessment (Phase 0 in the plan).

## Learner deliverables

- Explained career recommendation and a personal learning roadmap.
- At least one assessed introductory portfolio unit in the chosen direction, with feedback and a revision/reassessment route.
- Portfolio explanation plus CV/profile or mock-interview feedback.
- A dated career action plan: suitable application/referral when an actual opportunity exists, or agreed further preparation and a follow-up date.

The introductory unit is not a full occupational qualification. Participation does not guarantee employment, institutional credit, official certification, or an employer interview.

## Timing and staffing

Begin on a mutually agreed date after readiness checks. Allow two weeks for the introductory work, staffed review within three working days of a complete submission, and a scheduled career clinic. Follow up voluntarily at 30, 60 and 90 days. Expected learner effort is published per unit; the SOC introductory unit is approximately 10–14 hours.

Before invitations, name the Areté delivery owner, the partner coordinator, pathway reviewers and their backups, and the career reviewer. Agree the academic/work schedules, support channel and response hours. Pause recruitment when capacity is full.

## Budget to agree before launch

Planning allowance for 15 learners: 30 hours across marking, moderation and career review; six hours calibration; eight hours coordination; two hours orientation/event preparation; and six hours learner support: **52 staff hours**, plus measured infrastructure and any access assistance. Obtain agreed rates and replace these estimates with a priced budget before accepting payment or sponsor funding. The existing learner pass is not assumed to fund these services.

The written scope must identify who pays for each item and whether participation is sponsored, paid or free. Formal certification/exam fees, if later relevant, require separate confirmed terms.

## Evidence of value and data sharing

Report invited and activated learners, completed assessed units, useful revisions, career reviews delivered, learner feedback, actual opportunities/referrals and measured delivery costs. Distinguish application, interview, offer and work-start outcomes. The partner receives aggregate reporting; individual work or contact sharing requires the learner's specific permission.

## Next decision

Hold a short discovery meeting to confirm audience, responsibilities, readiness, dates, budget, privacy terms and success measures. If both parties see a fit, record the bounded scope and schedule the orientation.
