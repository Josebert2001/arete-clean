# Security Policy

## Supported deployment and scope

Only the current deployment of Areté is supported. Reports may concern the live site, the `api/*` serverless functions, and Supabase policies maintained in this repository.

## Report privately

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Josebert2001/arete-clean/security/advisories/new) as the primary channel. If it is unavailable, email [robertsunday333@gmail.com](mailto:robertsunday333@gmail.com). Please do not open a public issue for an exploitable finding.

Include the affected URL or component, steps to reproduce, expected and observed behavior, potential impact, and a safe proof of concept. Remove credentials and other people's data from screenshots and logs.

We aim to acknowledge a report within 72 hours and provide a status update at least weekly. We aim to fix critical issues within 7 days, high severity issues within 30 days, and lower severity issues within 90 days, subject to verification and deployment constraints. We will coordinate disclosure with the reporter after a fix is available.

We will not take legal action against good-faith research conducted within this policy. Please avoid accessing other students' data, changing live records, or running load or denial-of-service tests. The intentionally solvable CTF flags in the Security track are learning content, not vulnerabilities.
